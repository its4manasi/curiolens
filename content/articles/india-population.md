Population is often discussed as a single headline number. But the more useful questions are about *where* people live, how old they are, how quickly different regions are changing, and what that means for public services.

CurioLens can keep the argument and interpretation in this article file while showing selected public indicators dynamically.

## Why this setup matters

The article itself remains yours permanently as a plain Markdown file. The public-data component is separate, so it can be refreshed, replaced or expanded without rewriting the article.

Later, this page could include state comparisons, maps, historical charts and methodology notes.
